03 — Merit Order Engine

Marginal Cost Formula

MC = (HR × Pgas / 1000) + VOM
HR = heat rate (Btu/kWh)  ·  Pgas = $/MMBtu
VOM = variable O&M ($/MWh)  ·  ÷1000 converts kBtu → MMBtu

04 — Hourly Dispatch Algorithm

1

Load hour → datetime_utc

Iterate over all 168 hourly timestamps in the date window. Look up load_mw from the EIA demand series.

2

Resolve gas price → forward-fill

Match current date to price index. If no entry (weekend/holiday), use most recent prior price. Fallback: $3.50/MMBtu.

3

Compute net load → gas residual

Subtract all non-gas generation (nuclear, hydro, solar, wind, geo, coal, oil, other) from total load. Clip at 0.

4

Build merit order → sort by MC

Compute marginal cost for each of the 689 plants using current gas price. Sort cheapest → most expensive.

5

Walk the stack → dispatch

Dispatch plants in merit order until net load is met. Last plant dispatched sets the clearing price.

6

Record results → append row

Append hour summary to results list: load, net load, gas dispatched, clearing price, renewables offset, gas price.

06 — Trading Applications

Gas Demand Signal

Implied Burn → Basis Trade

  • gas_dispatched_mw × heat_rate / 1,000,000 = MMBtu/hr
  • High burn → bullish SoCal / PG&E basis vs HH
  • Go long SoCal–HH basis when net load is elevated
  • Current avg implied burn: ~55,000 MMBtu/hr
Power–Gas Spread

Spark Spread Monitor

  • Spark spread = power price − (gas × heat_rate / 1000)
  • Model clearing ≠ actual power → mean reversion trade
  • Model clearing currently: $116–174/MWh
  • Compare to CAISO day-ahead prices for divergence
Renewables Crowding

Short Basis on Solar Surge

  • renewables_offset % spikes → gas demand collapses
  • Bearish for near-term SoCal citygate basis
  • High-solar periods (spring midday) are predictable
  • Current avg offset: 64.6% — historically elevated

Model Limitations to Price In

  • Gas price is monthly lagged — not real-time
  • No transmission constraints (single-bus model)
  • No battery storage dispatch (suppresses clearing price)
  • Plant data from EIA-860 2023 — new builds excluded
